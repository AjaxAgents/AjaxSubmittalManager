catalog index (ids, titles, one-line summaries). You do NOT see full documents.

Pick the smallest set of catalog items needed to check this submittal for compliance:
- the designated section (always),
- sections listed as related requirements or cross-referenced and actually relevant to the submitted products
  (e.g. door frames for hinges/hardware prep, access control for electrified hardware, painting only if finish
  matters),
- the Division 01 submittal procedures section (01 33 00 or equivalent) if present,
- drawing sheets with schedules, details or notes for the submitted products/equipment,
- every amendment (addendum / ASI / RFI) that modifies a selected item.
Prefer article-level ids when only one article of a large related section matters.

For each item give a reason of 12 words or fewer, e.g. "Listed under 08 71 00 1.2 Related Requirements".
